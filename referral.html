The Referral Program

Share your link. When the people you refer — and the people they refer — get work done, you earn AgenticCore Points.

What are AgenticCore Points?

1 Point = $1 of credit toward any AgenticCore service. Points aren't cash and can't be withdrawn — they're a discount on future work, for you or anyone you choose to use them for. They never expire.

This is a rewards program, not an investment. You earn Points only when real, paid work is completed — never for simply signing up or referring someone who hasn't ordered anything yet.

Three levels, decreasing rewards

L1

Someone you referred directly

You earn 20% of their task value in Points, on their first 3 completed tasks. They also get 10% off those tasks.

L2

Someone they referred

You earn 10% of that task's value in Points, on their first 3 completed tasks.

L3

Someone that person referred

You earn 5% of that task's value in Points, on their first 3 completed tasks.

A worked example

Scenario

You refer Sara. Sara refers Ali. Ali refers Nadia.

Nadia orders a $500 website and it's completed.

What happens

Ali (referred Nadia directly, Level 1 from Nadia's perspective) earns $100 in Points (20% of $500).

Sara (Level 2 from Nadia's perspective) earns $50 in Points (10% of $500).

You (Level 3 from Nadia's perspective) earn $25 in Points (5% of $500).

This repeats for Nadia's first 3 completed tasks — after that, further tasks from Nadia no longer generate referral Points up the chain.

How to find your link

Every account has a unique referral link, available directly from your dashboard. A dedicated Referrals section shows your direct and indirect referrals at every level, along with your current Points balance.
