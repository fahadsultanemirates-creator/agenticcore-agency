Our Services

Comprehensive care under one roof. We will always discuss costs and alternatives before treatment begins.

  • Preventive Care

    Exams, professional cleanings, fluoride, sealants, and oral cancer screenings.

  • Fillings & Restorations

    Tooth-colored fillings, crowns, and bridges to restore comfort and function.

  • Cosmetic Dentistry

    Whitening, bonding, and veneers for a brighter, more even smile.

    Relieve pain and save natural teeth with careful, modern endodontic care.

  • Periodontal Treatment

    Gum evaluations, scaling and root planing, and ongoing periodontal maintenance.

  • Emergency Visits

    Same-day help for toothache, broken teeth, lost fillings, and swelling.
