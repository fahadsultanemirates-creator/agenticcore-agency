The dashboard, explained

How AgenticCore actually works

One dashboard. Five sections. No guessing what happens after you sign up.

01

My Projects — your home screen

This is what you see the moment you log in. Every project you have — active, awaiting your review, or already delivered — is listed here with a clear status, so you never have to message us just to ask "where's my project at?"

  • See status at a glance: In Progress, Awaiting your review, Revision requested, or Delivered
  • Tap into any project to see full detail, files, and the conversation history behind it
  • Nothing here needs a support ticket — it's designed to answer the question before you have to ask it
02

New Request — where a project actually starts

Describe what you need in plain language — no forms, no jargon. An AI agent asks a few follow-up questions to pin down exactly what you're getting, then presents a clear, written summary for you to confirm before anything is charged.

  • The agent confirms the exact scope and price based on what you describe — nothing is assumed
  • Nothing starts until you've approved the scope in writing — this protects you from surprises and us from misunderstandings
  • Once confirmed and paid, your project gets a name automatically and appears in My Projects
  • Every finalized request also reaches a real person directly — while our automated project manager is still being perfected, a human confirms and oversees every task personally
03

Services — everything we offer, without the clutter

Rather than a page crammed with every service and price, Services is a simple selector. Pick a category, see the price, and jump straight into a request when you're ready.

  • Websites, marketing, bookkeeping, audits & feasibility reports, and more — organized by category, not buried in a wall of text
  • Full marketing services are handled by our dedicated marketing arm, AgenticCore Biz — linked directly from here
04

Billing — everything in one place

Pay directly from the dashboard — no separate invoicing portal to dig through. Every service follows the same split — 30% upfront to begin work, 70% on completion — so you're never paying a large amount blindly before seeing real progress.

05

Business Pool — for teams who work with us often

Once your spend with us crosses a set threshold, you're automatically eligible for a separate, elevated experience — faster delivery, a dedicated human manager, and real discounts. Read the full breakdown →
