Payment & Delivery Policy

How long work takes, and how payment works — for every tier and every AgenticCore Package.

Delivery timeframes

  • Simple services are typically delivered within 24 hours
  • Heavier builds — full websites and custom frameworks — can take up to a couple of weeks
  • Some services run on an ongoing monthly basis rather than a single delivery

Payment

Every tier and every AgenticCore Package follows the same structure: 30% upfront to begin work, and the remaining 70% on completion. There's no separate pricing model for packages — the split is identical across the board.

Review before handover

Before your final payment, we show you the finished work for review — this is not full handover. Once the remaining 70% is paid, you get complete handover: files, access, and ownership, in full.

This protects both sides — you see exactly what you're paying for before the balance is due, and we're never left having delivered finished work with no payment behind it.
